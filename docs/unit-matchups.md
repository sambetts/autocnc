# Unit matchups, measured

Generated by `scripts/duel-lab.ps1` from 2130 duels over 3 world seed(s), played by the engine on open ground
(Tiberian Dawn rules as loaded by the autocnc mod, engine targeting restored for the lab only).
Every number below is the mean of fights that happened, not arithmetic on the rules. See
`tools/DuelLab/README.md` for the setup and its limits.

The equal-cost result differed between seeds in 30 of 380 ordered pairs. That means a win in one seed and
a loss or an unfinished fight in another, which also covers a sweep where one straggler survived. The median
margin across those pairs is 0.16. `matchups.csv` lists each pair's wins and margin spread.

**Margin** = share of the opponent's value destroyed minus share of your own value destroyed.
+1.0 is a clean sweep, -1.0 is being swept, 0 an even trade. Bold is a decisive win (>= +0.5).

- **1v1**: one of each, 18 cells apart, both attack-moving.
- **Equal cost**: 3,600 credits a side, so splash, crushing and numbers count.
- **With vision**: the same, but both sides see the whole lane, so long-range units can use
  their full reach. The gap between the two is what scouting and spotting are worth.
